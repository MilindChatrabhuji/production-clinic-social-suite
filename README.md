## Executive Overview
The **Production Clinic Social Media Management Suite** is engineered for medical clinics, private physician practices, hospital departments, and certified healthcare practitioners. It eliminates physician burnout by automating content research, multi-platform formatting, HIPAA compliance scanning, and patient appointment conversion attribution.

---

## Core Capabilities
1. **100% Evidence-Based Medicine**: Cites peer-reviewed literature (PubMed, JAMA, NEJM, Lancet) across cardiology, metabolic health, hypertension, sleep medicine, and preventive oncology screenings (strictly 0 alternative medicine claims).
2. **Multi-Platform Calendar Generator**: Tailored 30-day and 90-day schedules across LinkedIn, Instagram/Threads, X/Twitter, Facebook, and YouTube Community with character optimization and peak posting times.
3. **HIPAA & Medical Board Compliance Guardian**: Automated pre-publish scanner verifying zero patient PHI, enforcing statutory educational disclaimers, and blocking unverified therapeutic cure claims.
4. **Edge Webhook Dispatcher**: Cloudflare Workers edge router for automated multi-channel social webhooks with HMAC signature security.
5. **Patient Acquisition Attribution & ROI Reporting**: Executive monthly analytics tracking reach curves, patient inquiry click-throughs, and consultation bookings with responsive vector SVG charts.
6. **Productized B2B Retainers**: Turnkey legal agreements, 7-phase intake briefs, white-label sales proposals, and dual-currency USD/INR invoicing engine supporting $500, $1,500, and $3,000/mo retainers.
